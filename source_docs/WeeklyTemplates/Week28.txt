This week’s grammar focus: see, look, watch  
A1+ to A2- Progression This is the core rule.
- See = passive
- Look = intentional search
- Watch = intentional + time + change

Examples:
- I see bananas
- I look for bananas 
- I watch for banana sales
- I see the patient.
- I look for the chart.
- I watch the patient’s breathing.
- I see a tornado.
- I look for a tornado.
- I watch the weather on TV.
