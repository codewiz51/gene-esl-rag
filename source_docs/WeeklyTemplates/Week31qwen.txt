#BEGIN SECTION: VERB_FOCUS
============================================================

Weekly Verb Focus: say vs tell vs speak
- Say = Produce words; no directed recipient. I said "gracias." Say + that + quoted speech.
- Tell = Convey information to a person. I told her the news. Requires an indirect object. Tell + person + that.
- Speak = General use of a language; formal register. She speaks English and Spanish. No that-clause. Pairs with in (a language), to (formal addressee).

#END SECTION: VERB_FOCUS
