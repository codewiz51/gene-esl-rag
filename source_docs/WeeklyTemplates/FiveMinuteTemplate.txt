#BEGIN RULES: LANGUAGE_LEVEL
Target level: A2 → A2+. It's a 5-minute quick lesson.
This applies to every section of the document,
not only the Mini Story.
Use simple present, simple past, going to, can, should.
Avoid present perfect, past perfect. Simple first
conditional is fine (e.g. "If she is late, she
takes the bus alone.").
Keep sentences concrete. Avoid comparative
phrasing ("like a suggestion"), idiomatic time
expressions ("the moment the alert sounds"), and
abstract descriptions of grammar itself (what a
word "means," "carries," or "invites"). Explain
what a character does, not what a word represents.
Avoid long medical explanations.
#END RULES: LANGUAGE_LEVEL
