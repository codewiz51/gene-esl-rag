# Five-Minute Lesson Template (Claude)

Claude's spec for the afternoon Five-Minute review. The qwen files (FiveMinuteTemplate.txt, promptFive.md) stay as the offline backup and are not used here.

Write it only from the finished Main lesson. The learners, language level, characters, Cuban register, and conventions are in ClaudeMainTemplate.md and apply here too. It is a five-minute review for a tired student: short, concrete, nothing new.

## Source

- Each day compresses that day's Main story: same characters, setting, and events. No new events, characters, or details, and nothing borrowed from another day.
- Vocabulary comes from that day's Main vocabulary only.

## Layout

Day headings are `# MONDAY` etc. Days are separated by a blank line, a `---` line, and a blank line; `---` appears nowhere else. No bold, no `###`, no bullets. Leave a blank line between a label and the list after it.

```
# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. English word – palabra en español

## Grammar Focus

One to three sentences.

## Mini Story

1. Sentence.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. ...
2. ...

English → Spanish

1. ...

## Student Questions

1. Question? (hint)
2. Question? (hint)

---

# TUESDAY
```

## Section rules

Vocabulary Review
- The first four items of that day's Main vocabulary, in order (all of them if fewer than four).

Grammar Focus
- One to three plain sentences on one grammar point that appears in that day's Mini Story.
- A character is the subject ("Marisol asks politely with 'could you'"), never the word itself ("'could' means...") and never "We use...".
- The tense or form it names must match the sentences it quotes.

Mini Story
- 4-6 numbered sentences, English only, 8-12 words each.
- Simple present or simple past only. Connectors: and, but, because.

Translation Practice
- Spanish → English: exactly 2. English → Spanish: exactly 1.
- Each a full sentence of 8-12 words, worded to match the Main story.

Student Questions
- Exactly 2: one comprehension, one personal.
- Hint: one Spanish word or short phrase that helps her answer, never the answer itself.
