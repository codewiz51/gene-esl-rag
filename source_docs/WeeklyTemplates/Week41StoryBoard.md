# === WEEK NOTES ===
Setting: Family Health Clinic (Clínica de Salud Familiar), 1506 S. Agnew Ave, Oklahoma City. Hours: Monday to Friday 9:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Saturday closed; Sunday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Walk-ins are welcome and are seen in the order they arrive. Staff and provider speak Spanish and English. Doris Pérez is the primary provider. She is a nurse practitioner, and everyone calls her "Doctora." In English text she is Dr. Pérez. Letia is the LPN.
Cast for this week: Camy, Marisa and Donna appear on Monday morning. Tuesday, Thursday and Friday are clinic days, and every conversation is a clinic or work conversation. Wednesday is errands after work. On Saturday, Marisol spends the day with Camy, Marisa and Donna, and Crystal calls from the clinic. On Sunday, Marisol is at home with Camy.
Marisol's situation: this is her third full-time week at the clinic. She is calmer now, and she knows what to do when something is not normal.
Scope of practice: Marisol measures, asks questions, writes the patient's words in the chart, and tells Letia or Dr. Pérez. She never names a disease.

1 - Monday is not Marisol's carpool day. Marisa drives Camy and Donna to school.
2 - Marisol feels a little off this morning. She did not sleep well, but she arrives at the clinic at 9:00.
3 - She walks in and says, "Good morning! Let's get the ball rolling." It is a phrase she learned from Rosie.
4 - At 9:30, a patient, Mrs. Carter, is waiting at the front desk. She tells Marisol, "I feel light-headed, and my heart is beating fast."
5 - Marisol remembers from her training that light-headedness and a fast heartbeat can be warning signs. She stays calm and says, "Hang tight. Please sit down."
6 - Marisol takes her pulse: 112 beats per minute. That is above the normal range of 60 to 100.
7 - She tells Letia right away. She writes a short note in the chart: "Light-headed, fast pulse, started this morning."
8 - Dr. Pérez sees Mrs. Carter next. Marisol goes back to the front desk and says, "Okay, who's next?"

# === END VOCAB THURSDAY ===

1 - Thursday morning, the electronic blood pressure machine has a blinking red light. The battery is dead.
2 - Marisol sighs and says, "Okay, old-school today." She brings the manual cuff and the stethoscope from the cabinet.
3 - The first patient is Yoel Martínez, a man in his thirties. He walks slowly. His wife brings his medication list.
4 - Yoel tells Marisol that he has bad joint pain in his knees, ankles and wrists. He could not work for the last three days. Marisol sees swelling in his wrists. His temperature is normal.
5 - Marisol uses the manual cuff. A baby cries in the next room, and loud noise can give a wrong reading, so she waits for the room to be quiet. The first tapping sound is the top number (systolic). When the sound stops, that is the bottom number (diastolic). The reading is 124 over 78. (Mometrix CMA study guide, Blood Pressure, PDF page 28; Practice Test #1, question 165.)
6 - Marisol asks, "Did you travel recently?" Yoel says, "I came from Pinar del Río four months ago. Many people in my neighborhood had chikungunya."
7 - Marisol writes his words in the chart. She does not name a disease. She tells Dr. Pérez right away.
8 - Marisol thinks about her oldest brother, Jorge, in Cuba. He and his wife live in a nice house near where they grew up. The electricity goes out for many hours, so the refrigerator is useless. Food stays covered in the pot for two days, and the flies can be bad. She worries, but she keeps working so she can help her family.
9 - Dr. Pérez says, "Good question about the travel."

1. On Friday afternoon, a mother rushes in with her little boy, Andrés. He is four years old.
2. Andrés has a fever and a light cough. A red rash starts on his face and is spreading down his neck.
3. Marisol knows the clinic rule for a fever with a rash. She brings a small face mask for Andrés and one for his mother. She takes them to a private room and closes the door.
4. Her study guide says some germs stay in the air and travel far, so the room door stays closed. (Mometrix CMA study guide, Infection Control, PDF page 58.)
5. The mother is scared. Marisol tells her in a calm voice, "We're going to take care of him."
6. Marisol asks for his vaccine record. The mother finds a photo of the card on her phone.
7. Marisol tells Dr. Pérez right away. Dr. Pérez examines Andrés and says, "We need to test him and call the health department."
8. Marisol washes her hands and feels proud. She did every step in order.
