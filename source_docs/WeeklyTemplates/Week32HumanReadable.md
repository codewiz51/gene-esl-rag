# === END VOCAB THURSDAY ===

Reinforce Do, Make, Create.

Marisol meets with a new patient, a
36‑year‑old woman with one child, high
blood pressure, and clear signs of stress.
The woman is overweight, she smokes,
and she avoids eye contact when Marisol
gently asks about alcohol. The patient
says she “only drinks socially,” but her
tone makes Marisol think she might be
minimizing the truth.
Marisol does her assessment slowly,
asking simple questions. She makes
notes about the patient’s blood pressure
and weight. She explains that exercise
helps the heart, even if it’s just walking 20
minutes a day. She talks about smoking,
how it damages the lungs and raises
blood pressure. The patient nods but
looks embarrassed.
Marisol continues. She explains that
substance abuse—even mild overuse—
can affect sleep, mood, and weight. She
describes how nutrition plays a role in
energy and blood pressure. She tells the
patient that weight control is not about
perfection; it’s about small changes.
The woman sighs and says, “I don’t know
if I can do all that.”
Marisol smiles. “You don’t have to do
everything today. But you can make one
change this week.”
The patient thinks for a moment and says
she will try to do a short walk every
morning.
