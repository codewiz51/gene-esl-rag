# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. blood pressure cuff – manguito de presión
2. thermometer – termómetro
3. stethoscope – estetoscopio
4. take a seat – tomar asiento

## Grammar Focus

Marisol takes his blood pressure, and then she takes his temperature. Marisol is "she," so the verb is "takes."

## Mini Story

1. Monday is carpool day, and Marisol drives the girls to school.
2. At the clinic, the waiting room is full before nine.
3. Marisol tells an elderly man, "Please wait a moment."
4. He asks to take a seat closer to the door.
5. Marisol takes his blood pressure with the cuff and his temperature.
6. She writes the numbers on the iPad before Dra. Pérez comes.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Un señor mayor está parado cerca de la puerta con su bastón.
2. Marisol le toma la presión con el manguito al señor.

English → Spanish

1. She listens to his lungs with the stethoscope.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol say to the elderly man? (esperar)
2. Do you take a seat when you wait at the doctor's office? (tomar asiento)

# TUESDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. results – resultados
2. check the results / look into it – revisar los resultados (formal / casual)
3. grocery list – lista de compras
4. electric bill – cuenta de electricidad

## Grammar Focus

Marisol checks the grocery list, and then she checks the electric bill. Rosie gives her an easier way to say it: "I'll look into it."

## Mini Story

1. After work, Marisol checks the grocery list before the store.
2. The price of chicken and eggs went up again.
3. At home, she checks the electric bill and she sighs.
4. Rosie texts her and asks about the patient's results.
5. Marisol answers Rosie, and she promises to check the results.
6. Rosie tells her to say "I'll look into it," like on TV.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. En la tienda, revisa el precio del pollo y de los huevos.
2. En la casa, revisa la cuenta de electricidad y suspira.

English → Spanish

1. Rosie asks if the clinic got the patient's results back.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol check at the store? (precio)
2. Do you check your electric bill every month? (cuenta de electricidad)

# THURSDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. pulse oximeter – oxímetro de pulso
2. otoscope – otoscopio
3. recovering / bouncing back – recuperándose (formal / casual)
4. monitor the patient / keep an eye on them – vigilar al paciente / estar pendiente de él (formal / casual)

## Grammar Focus

Marisol explains the plan to the patient. When he looks confused, she explains it again with easier words.

## Mini Story

1. A patient comes back to the clinic for his broken arm.
2. Marisol takes his vitals and checks the pulse oximeter reading.
3. Dra. Pérez tells him that he is recovering well.
4. Marisol explains the plan, but the man looks confused.
5. Amanda whispers an easier phrase, and Marisol explains it again.
6. Before he leaves, Marisol checks his ear with the otoscope.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol le pone la pinza chiquita en el dedo al señor.
2. La Dra. Pérez le dice que se está recuperando bien.

English → Spanish

1. The doctor wants us to monitor your arm for two more weeks.

## Student Questions

1. What does Amanda whisper to Marisol? (hinchazón)
2. Do you explain things two ways when people look confused? (explicar)

# FRIDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. front desk – recepción
2. co-pay – copago
3. the doctor is unavailable / the doctor is tied up – la doctora no está disponible / la doctora está ocupada (formal / casual)
4. confused – confundido

## Grammar Focus

Marisol tells the husband that the doctor is unavailable. Rosie tells her the casual way: "She's tied up."

## Mini Story

1. On a busy Friday, a patient's husband calls the clinic.
2. Marisol checks the whiteboard, and Dra. Pérez is with a patient.
3. Marisol tells him that the doctor is unavailable right now.
4. Rosie whispers that she can say the doctor is tied up.
5. At the front desk, Marisol explains the co-pay to a man.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El esposo de una paciente pide hablar con la Dra. Pérez.
2. Después le toma la tarjeta del seguro y le saca una copia.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol explains the co-pay to the confused man at the front desk.

## Student Questions

1. Why can't the husband talk to Dra. Pérez? (pizarra)
2. Do you understand your co-pay at the doctor's office? (copago)
