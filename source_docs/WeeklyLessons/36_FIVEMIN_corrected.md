# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. scraped knee – rodilla raspada
2. non-stick gauze pad – gasa que no se pega
3. gauze tape – cinta para gasa
4. tears – lágrimas

## Grammar Focus

Marisol cleans the knee and wraps it with gauze tape, and she describes each step in the simple past. Because the crying takes time, they are late to school.

## Mini Story

1. Camy and Donna run outside to Marisa's van.
2. Camy does a cartwheel on the sidewalk for Donna.
3. Her foot hits a stone and she falls hard.
4. Marisol cleans the scraped knee with warm water.
5. She wraps it with gauze tape and a pad.
6. They are late to school because of the crying.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol limpia la rodilla raspada con agua tibia.
2. Camy y Donna corren afuera a la van de Marisa.

English → Spanish

1. She wraps the knee with gauze tape and a pad.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol put on Camy's scraped knee? (gasa que no se pega)
2. Have you ever been late to school because of an accident? (un accidente)
