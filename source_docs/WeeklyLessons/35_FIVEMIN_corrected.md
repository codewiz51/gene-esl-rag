# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. migraine – migraña
2. refill a prescription – renovar la receta
3. symptom – síntoma
4. waiting room – sala de espera

## Grammar Focus

Mrs. Ruiz has to go to work. Dra. Pérez has to see her first.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol drives Camy and Donna because Marisa's car is at the mechanic.
2. The waiting room is full and it smells like old coffee.
3. Mrs. Ruiz has a migraine and holds her glasses in one hand.
4. She needs a refill but Dra. Pérez has to see her first.
5. Mrs. Ruiz sighs and closes her eyes because she has to work.
6. Marisol understands because for many women no work means no pay.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. La sala de espera está llena y huele a café viejo.
2. La señora Ruiz tiene migraña pero tiene que ir a trabajar.

English → Spanish

1. She needs a refill but the doctor has to see her first.

## Student Questions

1. What does Mrs. Ruiz need from the clinic? (renovar la receta)
2. What do you do when you have a bad headache? (tomar una pastilla)

# TUESDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. follow-up – cita de control
2. recovery – recuperación
3. without pain – sin dolor

## Grammar Focus

Mr. Vega says he can walk without pain. Marisol says she can tell the doctor.

## Mini Story

1. Tuesday is quiet and the fan is the only sound.
2. Mr. Vega comes in for a follow-up after knee surgery.
3. He says he can walk to the store without pain.
4. Marisol smiles and says she is not the doctor.
5. Dra. Pérez checks his knee and says his recovery is good.
6. He has one more follow-up in two weeks.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El señor Vega puede caminar hasta la tienda sin dolor.
2. Su recuperación va bien y tiene una cita de control más.

English → Spanish

1. Mr. Vega can walk to the store without pain.

## Student Questions

1. What can Mr. Vega do without pain? (caminar)
2. What can you do to rest after work? (descansar)

# WEDNESDAY

2. take a pill – tomarse una pastilla

4. brush your teeth – cepillarse los dientes

## Grammar Focus

# THURSDAY

## Vocabulary Review

2. get off the bus – bajarse de la guagua
3. over-the-counter – sin receta
4. get it – entender

## Grammar Focus

The young man gets off the bus at the wrong stop. Dra. Pérez says he will get better.

## Mini Story

2. He tripped on the curb and hurt his ankle.

Spanish → English

1. El muchacho se bajó de la guagua en la parada equivocada.
2. La doctora dice que se va a mejorar en dos semanas.

English → Spanish

1. He can get medicine over the counter because it is cheaper.

## Student Questions

1. Why does the young man hurt his ankle? (la parada equivocada)
2. Where do you buy medicine without a prescription? (farmacia)

# SUNDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. porch – portal
2. get good news – recibir buenas noticias
3. weekend shift – turno de fin de semana
4. migraine – migraña

## Grammar Focus

Marisa asks, "Did you get good news?" Marisa says they can walk every Saturday.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol and Marisa sit on the porch while the girls run.
2. Marisa asks if Marisol got good news, and Marisol says yes.
3. There are no more weekend shifts for Marisol at the clinic.
4. Marisol tells Marisa about the woman with the migraine.
5. Marisa says they can walk together every Saturday.
6. Marisol will have more time for Camy and for her English.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol y Marisa se sientan en el portal a hablar.
2. Marisol recibió buenas noticias sobre sus turnos de fin de semana.

English → Spanish

1. They can walk together every Saturday because Marisol has time off.

## Student Questions

1. What good news does Marisol tell Marisa? (turnos de fin de semana)
2. Where do you like to sit and talk with a friend? (portal)
