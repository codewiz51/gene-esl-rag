# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. a little off – no muy bien / un poco rara
2. let's get the ball rolling – vamos a empezar / manos a la obra
3. light-headed – mareado / mareada
4. warning sign – señal de alerta

## Grammar Focus

Marisol feels a little off because she did not sleep well. She tells the reader why she is not okay. Her body and her mind both say she needs rest.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol feels a little off because she did not sleep well.
2. She arrives at the clinic and says, "Let's get the ball rolling."
3. Mrs. Carter feels light-headed and her heart beats fast.
4. Marisol takes her pulse and tells Letia right away.
5. Dr. Pérez sees Mrs. Carter, and Marisol goes back to the desk.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol se siente un poco rara porque no durmió bien.
2. La señora Carter se siente mareada y su corazón late rápido.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol le toma el pulso y le dice a Letia de inmediato.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol write in Mrs. Carter's chart? (señal de alerta)
2. Do you ever feel a little off in the morning? (un poco rara)

# WEDNESDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. ATM – cajero automático
2. groceries – la compra / los mandados
3. next in line / who's up next – el próximo / ¿quién sigue? (formal / casual)
4. receipt – recibo

## Grammar Focus

Marisol says "Who's up next?" at the clinic every day. The cashier says the same thing at the store. Marisol smiles because the two places feel the same.

## Mini Story

1. After work, Marisol stops at the ATM and goes to the store.
2. Camy calls and says she needs poster board for school.
3. The line is long, and the cashier asks, "Who's up next?"
4. Marisol checks her receipt and feels ready for Thursday.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol va al cajero automático y luego a la tienda.
2. Camy necesita cartulina para su proyecto de ciencias.

English → Spanish

1. La cajera pregunta quién sigue en la fila.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol check in the car after the store? (recibo)
2. Do you say "who's up next?" anywhere in your life? (¿quién sigue?)

# THURSDAY

3. old-school – a la antigua

## Grammar Focus

Marisol thinks about Jorge in Cuba but keeps working. She worries about him, and her hands stay on the patient. She chooses to focus on Yoel, not on her fear.

## Mini Story

Spanish → English

2. Yoel tiene dolor en las articulaciones de sus rodillas.

English → Spanish

# FRIDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. rash – sarpullido
2. spreading – regándose / extendiéndose
3. face mask – nasobuco
4. private room – cuarto aparte

## Grammar Focus

Marisol tells the mother, "We're going to take care of him." Her voice is calm, and the mother stops crying. Marisol's plan is clear and kind.

## Mini Story

1. A mother rushes in with her four-year-old son, Andrés.
2. Andrés has a fever, a light cough, and a red rash.
3. Marisol brings a face mask for Andrés and his mother.
4. She takes them to a private room and closes the door.
5. Dr. Pérez says they need to test him and call the department.
6. Marisol washes her hands and feels proud of every step.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El sarpullido se extiende por el cuello del niño.
2. Marisol lleva un nasobuco para Andrés y su madre.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol los lleva a un cuarto aparte y cierra la puerta.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol ask the mother for? (tarjeta de vacunas)
2. Do you feel proud when you do every step in order? (orgullo)

# SUNDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. light-headed – mareado / mareada
2. stomach cramps – retortijones / cólicos
3. stethoscope – estetoscopio
4. call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma

## Grammar Focus

Marisol feels calm because she chose her daughter. She made the right choice on Saturday, and it stays with her. Camy brings coffee, and Marisol lets the worry go.

## Mini Story

1. Sunday morning has no alarm, and Marisol drinks coffee on the porch.
2. She remembers Mrs. Carter's fast pulse and Mr. Delgado's pale face.
3. She thinks about the dead battery and the manual cuff on Thursday.
4. Camy brings her a second cup of coffee.
5. Marisol feels calm because she chose her daughter, and it was right.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol recuerda que la señora Carter se sintió mareada.
2. El estetoscopio y el manguito manual fueron necesarios el jueves.

English → Spanish

1. Carla se reportó enferma otra vez, y Marisol dijo que no.

## Student Questions

1. What does Camy bring to Marisol on Sunday morning? (café)
2. Do you feel calm when you choose your family first? (mi familia)
