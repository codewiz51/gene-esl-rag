# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. computer class – clase de computación
2. sniffles – moqueo (la nariz que gotea)
3. chest congestion – pecho cargado / congestión en el pecho (casual / formal)
4. oxygen level – nivel de oxígeno

## Grammar Focus

Marisol uses the thermometer first. Marisol is "she," so the verb is "uses."

## Mini Story

1. Monday is Marisol's day to take the girls to school.
2. Camy is excited because she has computer class today.
3. Michael Lopez has the sniffles and complains of chest congestion.
4. Marisol uses the thermometer first, and he has a low fever.
5. The first time, the pulse oximeter shows no number.
6. His oxygen level is 95 percent, a little low but normal.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Camy dice que hoy tiene clase de computación, y está embullada.
2. Tiene moqueo y se queja de que tiene el pecho cargado.

English → Spanish

1. His blood pressure is a little high for a young man.

## Student Questions

1. Why is there no number on the pulse oximeter the first time? (manos)
2. Do your children use computers at school? (computadora)

# TUESDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. reading – lectura
2. systolic / diastolic – sistólica / diastólica (el número de arriba / el número de abajo)
3. cuff size – tamaño del manguito
4. a little high – un poco alta

## Grammar Focus

Marisol utilizes the blood pressure cuff for Mr. Rodríguez. Marisol is "she," so the verb is "utilizes."

## Mini Story

1. Marisol utilizes the blood pressure cuff for Mr. Rodríguez.
2. The cuff is too small, and she gets a bigger cuff.
3. The reading is 138 over 88, and it is a little high.
4. Mr. Rodríguez says he always gets nervous at the doctor.
5. Marisol tells him to take a deep breath and sit quietly.
6. The second reading is lower, and she tells Dr. Pérez.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El manguito es muy chiquito, y Marisol busca uno más grande.
2. El señor Rodríguez siempre se pone nervioso en el médico.

English → Spanish

1. The top number is systolic, and the bottom number is diastolic.

## Student Questions

1. Why does Marisol get a bigger cuff? (chiquito)
2. What do you do when you feel nervous? (respirar)

2. Do you use a fax machine at work? (fax)
