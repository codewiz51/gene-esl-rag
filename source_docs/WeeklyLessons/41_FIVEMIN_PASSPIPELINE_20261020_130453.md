# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. a little off – no muy bien / un poco rara
2. let's get the ball rolling – vamos a empezar / manos a la obra
3. light-headed – mareado / mareada
4. warning sign – señal de alerta

## Grammar Focus

Marisol uses "let's" to start the day with a phrase from Rosie. She says it at the clinic, and it still makes her feel silly.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol feels a little off because she did not sleep well.
2. She arrives at the clinic and says, "Let's get the ball rolling."
3. Mrs. Carter waits at the front desk and feels light-headed.
4. Marisol takes her pulse and tells Letia right away.
5. Dr. Pérez sees Mrs. Carter and Marisol goes back to the desk.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Me siento un poco rara porque no dormí bien anoche.
2. La paciente espera en la mesa y se siente mareada.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol says "let's get the ball rolling" to start the day.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol do when Mrs. Carter feels light-headed? (el pulso)
2. Do you ever feel a little off before work? (el sueño)

# TUESDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. stomach cramps – retortijones / cólicos
2. weak – débil
3. pale – pálido / pálida
4. check in – registrar al paciente

## Grammar Focus

Marisol uses "checks in" to register Mr. Delgado at the front desk. She does this before she gets Letia to help.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol arrives with coffee after a long night with Camy.
2. Mr. Delgado looks pale and holds his stomach at the desk.
3. He tells Marisol he has stomach cramps and feels weak.
4. Marisol says "No worries" and checks him in for Letia.
5. She takes his blood pressure and writes in the chart.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El paciente tiene retortijones y se siente débil.
2. Marisol dice "no se preocupe" y lo registra.

English → Spanish

1. Mr. Delgado looks pale and holds his stomach at the desk.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol do after Mr. Delgado tells her about his cramps? (registrar)
2. Do you ever feel weak after a long night? (dormir)

# FRIDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. rash – sarpullido
2. spreading – regándose / extendiéndose
3. face mask – nasobuco
4. private room – cuarto aparte

## Grammar Focus

Marisol "brings" a face mask to Andrés at the front desk, and then she "takes" them to a private room. She moves the mask toward the patient, and then away to the room.

## Mini Story

1. A mother brings her four-year-old son, Andrés, to the clinic.
2. He has a fever and a red rash on his face.
3. Marisol brings a face mask for Andrés and his mother.
4. She takes them to a private room and closes the door.
5. Dr. Pérez says to test him and call the health department.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El sarpullido se extiende por el cuello del niño.
2. Marisol trae un nasobuco para Andrés y su madre.

English → Spanish

1. She takes them to a private room and closes the door.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol do when she sees a rash with fever? (el nasobuco)
2. Do you ever wear a face mask at the doctor? (médico)

# SATURDAY

## Vocabulary Review

2. donuts – donuts
3. call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma
4. cover a shift – cubrir un turno

## Grammar Focus

Carla "calls in sick" to tell the clinic she will not come to work. Marisol uses "I already have plans" to say no and keep her day with Camy.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol stays in bed and Camy says she feels lazy.
2. Marisa and Donna bring donuts and Donna has sugar on her chin.
3. Marisol says "Next Saturday my treat!" and they plan a museum trip.
4. Crystal calls because Carla called in sick for the shift.
5. Marisol says "I already have plans" and feels a little guilty.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Carla se reporta enferma y no va a trabajar.
2. Marisol y Camy son dos gotas de agua.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol says "I already have plans" and feels a little guilty.

## Student Questions

1. Why does Marisol say no to Crystal? (el turno)
2. Do you ever say no to a friend because you have plans? (decir no)

# SUNDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. light-headed – mareado / mareada
2. stomach cramps – retortijones / cólicos
3. stethoscope – estetoscopio
4. call in sick – reportarse enfermo / enferma

## Grammar Focus

Marisol "remembers" her whole week while she drinks coffee on the porch. She thinks about Monday, Thursday, and Saturday in one calm moment.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol drinks coffee on the porch and remembers her week.
2. Monday, Mrs. Carter felt light-headed, and Tuesday, Mr. Delgado had cramps.
3. Thursday, the battery was dead, and she used the stethoscope.
4. Saturday, Carla called in sick, and Marisol said no.
5. Camy brings a second cup and Marisol feels calm.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. El lunes, la señora Carter se sintió mareada.
2. Carla se reporta enferma el sábado y Marisol dice no.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol drinks coffee on the porch and remembers her week.

## Student Questions

1. What does Marisol remember from Thursday? (el estetoscopio)
2. Do you ever sit and think about your whole week? (café)
