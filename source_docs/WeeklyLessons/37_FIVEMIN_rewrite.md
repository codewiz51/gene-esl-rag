# MONDAY

## Vocabulary Review

1. fender bender – un choquecito
2. traffic jam – un tranque
3. skit – una obrita de teatro
4. draw blood – extraer sangre

## Grammar Focus

Camy performs in a class skit, and Dra. Pérez performs several procedures. Each one is "she," so the verb has an -s.

## Mini Story

1. Marisol helps Camy practice her lines for the class skit.
2. Marisa drives Donna and Camy to school this morning.
3. They see a fender bender, and it causes a traffic jam.
4. Marisol hurries to work because she must draw blood at 8:00.
5. At the clinic, she assists Dra. Pérez and sets up the tray.
6. Dra. Pérez performs several procedures at the clinic today.

## Translation Practice

Spanish → English

1. Marisol le ayuda a Camy a ensayar el papel.
2. Marisa lleva a Donna y a Camy a la escuela.

English → Spanish

1. Marisol hurries to work because she must draw blood.

## Student Questions

1. Who drives Donna and Camy to school on Monday? (vecina)
2. Who drives your children to school when you cannot? (llevar)
