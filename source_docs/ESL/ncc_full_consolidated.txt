A Day in the Life of a Medical Assistant — Setting by Setting (Los Angeles Focus)

Typical Day by Workplace

Emergency Room / Trauma
A typical day in the ER is a 12-hour shift (often 3x12 or 4x10 schedule including nights). The MA cleans wounds, takes vital signs, transports patients, and stocks trauma rooms. Administrative duties are minimal; the focus is clinical throughput.

Hospital Departments (Pediatrics, Cardiology, OB/GYN, Oncology, Ortho, Outpatient Surgery)
A typical day runs Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The MA's duties vary by department. In Cardiology they operate EKG machines and run stress tests. In Pediatrics they give vaccines and soothe children during exams. In OB/GYN they assist with prenatal check-ups.

Primary Care / Family Medicine
A typical day is Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The MA takes vitals, administers vaccines, performs throat swabs, assists with minor procedures, processes prescription refills, and verifies insurance coverage.

Specialty Clinics (Dermatology, Ophthalmology, Allergy, Orthopedics)
A typical day is Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The MA assists with minor surgical procedures such as skin lesion removal or administers skin prick tests for allergy panels.

Home Health
The MA travels to patients' homes. A typical day involves tracking vitals, drawing blood, checking glucose levels, changing bandages, and verifying medication adherence and a safe home environment. No nights or weekends.

Skills and Qualities
Technical: vitals, blood draws, injections, sterilizing equipment, EHR documentation, medical terminology.
Interpersonal: empathy, attention to detail, teamwork, communication.

Challenges
Physically demanding (on feet for long hours, moving gurneys, lifting patients). Little downtime in busy settings. Germ exposure and needle-stick risk. Dealing with angry patients or demanding doctors.

Rewards
Flexibility in choosing work environment. Training in under one year. Meaningful, high-impact work.
California salary range: $39,290 to $77,300 per year.
