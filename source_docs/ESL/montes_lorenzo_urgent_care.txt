Lorenzo — A Day in the Life of a Medical Assistant at an Urgent Care Center

Lorenzo describes his typical day at an urgent care center in Gardena, California in the first person. He says, "We see everything from simple stomach bugs to traumatic injuries."

Morning tasks and start of shift:
Lorenzo starts his shift by stocking and cleaning exam rooms, setting up supplies for the procedures that day's patients will need. He prepares instruments, checks that all rooms are ready, and makes sure the waiting area is organized.

Middle of the day — clinical and hands-on duties:
As patients come through, Lorenzo takes vital signs, gives injections, draws lab samples, and assists the providers during procedures. His workload varies wildly from hour to hour — a stomach flu in one room, a broken bone or laceration in the next. He discharges patients with education materials and follow-up instructions, making sure they understand wound care, medication schedules, and when to come back.

End of shift:
Lorenzo finishes by cleaning and restocking all exam rooms, disposing of any biohazard waste, and handing off any incomplete documentation to the next shift.
