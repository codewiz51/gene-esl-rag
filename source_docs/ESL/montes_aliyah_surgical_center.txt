Aliyah — A Day in the Life of a Medical Assistant at an Outpatient Surgical Center

Aliyah describes her typical day at an outpatient surgical center in the first person. Her role is primarily front-office and administrative, which she calls "the eyes and ears of our clinic."

Morning tasks and start of shift:
Aliyah starts her morning by greeting the first wave of patients, pulling up their records, checking them into the system, and verifying insurance information. She coordinates the morning's surgical schedule across multiple doctors, confirming which procedures are set and which rooms are assigned.

Middle of the day — administrative and coordination duties:
As the day goes on, Aliyah's typical tasks include calling insurance companies to verify coverage, handling medical coding and billing for the procedures performed, scheduling follow-up appointments, and fielding phone calls from patients and referring providers. She stays at the front desk but is constantly moving between the phone, the EHR, and the patients in the waiting area.

End of shift:
Aliyah finishes her day by reconciling the day's billing, ensuring all coding is submitted, and preparing the next day's schedule. She describes herself as the operational backbone of the clinic.
