# Three Ideas For ESL Stories — Vetted Findings

*Vetted October 5, 2026. Original candidates from Gemma; each claim checked against the source.*

## 1. Alzheimer's blood tests — real, but the details are wrong

- **Bad link.** The URL goes to Medical News Today's news index, not the article. The real article is [here](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/fda-clears-two-new-alzheimers-blood-tests-including-one-for-adults-as-young-as-40), dated **Sept 26, 2026**, not "October."
- **Overstated summary.** Both tests are cleared only as aids for adults who already have cognitive symptoms (PrecivityAD2 for ages 40+, Elecsys pTau217 for ages 55+). The article says they are not for screening and not stand-alone diagnostic tests. So "screening early-stage decline" and "replace invasive procedures" are wrong. "Approved" should be "cleared."
- Roche's clearance was actually [Aug 24](https://www.roche.com/investors/updates/inv-update-2026-08-24), so only the C2N part falls within the last 30 days.

## 2. Salmonella in broccoli sprouts — accurate, the cleanest of the three

- The URL works, and the quote appears word for word on the [FDA page](https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/outbreak-investigation-salmonella-sprouts-september-2026).
- Details you could add: updated Sept 24, 32 people sick in 6 northwestern states (ID, MN, MT, OR, UT, WA), 3 hospitalized, no deaths. Grower: Evergreen Fresh Sprouts, LLC (Moyie Springs, ID).

## 3. COVID vaccines for children — real event, wrong source and wrong framing

- **Political source.** Balloon Juice is a partisan political blog, not a health source. A neutral replacement is [Drug Topics](https://www.drugtopics.com/view/cdc-opens-orders-for-covid-19-vaccines-in-vaccines-for-children), a pharmacy trade publication, dated Sept 29. [CIDRAP](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/covid-19/cdc-inaction-delays-covid-shots-low-income-children) also works.
- **"Vulnerable children" is wrong.** This is the Vaccines for Children (VFC) program, and eligibility is based on coverage: children on Medicaid, uninsured children, and American Indian/Alaska Native children. It has nothing to do with medical vulnerability.
- The delay itself is a political story, because the federal vaccine advisory committee (ACIP) hasn't met since December 2025. For a lesson, drop the delay and keep the practical fact: free vaccines through VFC, ask your clinic. That fact also matters to the students as parents, since many of their children likely qualify.

## What this shows about Gemma

Its quotes are reliable, but its URLs and summaries drift. Two checks would catch most of it:

- Require a link to the specific article, not an index page.
- Confirm the date on the page itself.
