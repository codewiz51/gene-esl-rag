Jaqui — A Day in the Life of a Medical Assistant at a Midwifery Clinic

Jaqui describes her typical day at a midwifery clinic in the first person. Her role blends front-end administrative work with hands-on clinical support for the midwife team.

Morning tasks and start of shift:
Jaqui starts her morning by reviewing the midwife team's appointments, pulling up patient records in advance, and prepping rooms for procedures such as Pap smears, ultrasounds, and fetal non-stress tests. She sets up the supplies each midwife will need for the day.

Middle of the day — patient flow and clinical duties:
As patients arrive, Jaqui takes vitals, collects urine samples for protein testing, gathers full medical histories, and condenses that information for the midwife. She serves as a chaperone during exams, administers flu shots and TDaP vaccines, and updates patient charts between visits. She describes herself as a key link between the patient and the provider.

End of shift:
Jaqui finishes her day by completing documentation in the EHR, reconciling any supplies used during procedures, and prepping the clinic for the next day's schedule.
